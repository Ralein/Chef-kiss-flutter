Chef's Kiss Mobile App

Privacy Policy

Effective Date: November 8, 2026

Chef's Kiss (“we”, “our”, or “the app”), developed by Ralein Nova, is an illustrated personal recipe journal and calm cooking companion. We respect your personal privacy and believe your recipes, meal notes, and kitchen memories belong solely to you.

1. Summary of Core Principles

  • Local-First: All recipes, cookbooks, notes, and custom shelf arrangements are stored locally on your device in a private SQLite database.
  • No Advertising or Tracking: We do not include third-party advertising SDKs, tracking pixels, or cross-app analytics brokers.
  • Your Data Stays Yours: We do not sell, rent, or monetize your personal data.

2. Information We Access and How It Is Used

A. Local Storage & Device Files

The app stores recipes, ingredients, instructions, cooking logs, book styles, and photos in your device's private app storage. We do not copy your recipes to any external marketing server.

B. Camera and Photos

When you explicitly grant camera or photo gallery permissions, images are used strictly to:

  • Set custom cover or spine graphics for your cookbooks.
  • Attach photos of cooked dishes to your cooking journal.
  • Scan printed recipe cards or dishes for text/ingredient extraction.

C. AI-Powered Recipe Extraction & Assistance

When you choose to use optional AI features (such as scanning a handwritten recipe, extracting ingredients from text/URL, or generating dish ideas), the necessary text or image is transmitted securely via encrypted HTTPS to our AI service provider solely to process the request and return structured recipe data. This data is not used for advertising or model training.

D. Audio & Speech Synthesis (TTS)

During Hands-Free Cooking Mode, the app uses your device's built-in text-to-speech engine to read recipe steps aloud. Processing occurs locally on your operating system.

3. Third-Party Services

The app connects to external endpoints only when performing user-initiated actions (e.g., querying AI models or importing public recipe webpages via URL). We do not integrate third-party ad networks or tracking brokers.

4. Data Retention & Deletion

Because data is stored on-device, you maintain total control. Deleting items inside the app or uninstalling the app permanently removes all stored data from your device.

5. Children's Privacy

Chef's Kiss is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

6. Security

All network communications (such as AI requests or URL recipe imports) are conducted exclusively over industry-standard encrypted channels (HTTPS / TLS).
