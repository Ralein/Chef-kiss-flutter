# Google Play listing: Chef’s Kiss

## App name (27/30)
Chef's Kiss: Recipe Journal

## Short description (78/80)
Keep your recipes on a cozy kitchen shelf. Cook step by step, scan, log meals.

## Full description (2822/4000)
Your recipes deserve better than screenshots, bookmarks and a stained notebook. Chef’s Kiss gives them a home: a warm, personal recipe book that looks like your own kitchen shelf, and a calm companion for when it’s time to cook.

A KITCHEN SHELF THAT’S YOURS
Your cookbooks stand on wooden shelves among pantry jars, utensil crocks, candles and trailing plants. Choose a look (Cozy kitchen, Herb garden, Bistro or Retro diner), then make it your own: move books and decorations around, change the wall and the wood, and give each book its own cover, spine colour and pattern.

A COOKBOOK YOU CAN OPEN
Tap a book and turn its pages. Every recipe gets its own keepsake spread with ingredients, method and chef’s tips, and a table of contents takes you straight to the one you want. Prefer something simpler? Switch to tidy recipe cards any time.

START WITH TESTED CLASSICS
Begin with eight real, tested recipes from around the world: Spaghetti Aglio e Olio, Shakshuka, Dal Tadka, Lemon Garlic Roast Chicken, Egg Fried Rice, Fluffy Pancakes, Banana Bread and Classic Guacamole. Each one goes on your shelf as its own book.

COOK STEP BY STEP
• Cook Mode shows one large, clear step at a time.
• Built-in timers for every step that needs one, with a gentle alert when they finish.
• Read-aloud: hear each step, so your hands can stay in the dough.
• Change the servings and every ingredient amount follows.
• Your screen stays on while you cook.

BRING ALL YOUR RECIPES TOGETHER
• Scan a cookbook page, a handwritten card or a screenshot, and it becomes a recipe.
• Import from a recipe website by pasting its link.
• Paste recipe text from notes, messages or email.
• Describe a dish and let Chef’s Kiss AI write the full recipe.
• Or simply write your own.

KNOW WHAT YOU’RE EATING
• Snap a meal to see estimated calories, macros and common allergens, then add it to your food log.
• Snap your fridge or groceries and get ideas for what to cook.
• See estimated nutrition for your recipes and track your day against goals you set.

FREE, PRIVATE AND YOURS
• No account, no sign-up and no ads.
• Your recipes, cookbooks, photos and food log stay on your phone.
• Export a backup whenever you like.
• Chef’s Kiss AI is free and needs no setup. When you use an AI feature, only what that feature needs is sent, never your whole recipe book.
• Light and dark themes follow your phone.

MADE BY ONE PERSON, WITH CARE
Chef’s Kiss is built by an independent developer who wanted one beautiful place for the recipes we love. Have an idea or found something that feels off? Get in touch from Settings → About. Every note helps shape what comes next.

Good to know: nutrition, allergen and food-safety information is an estimate for general guidance and isn’t medical advice. Always check labels and follow safe cooking temperatures.
